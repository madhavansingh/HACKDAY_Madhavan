# MedGuide – AI Health Companion

**Hackathon:** Hack Days, Bhopal

**Live Demo:** [https://hack-day-madhavan.vercel.app/](https://hack-day-madhavan.vercel.app/)

---

## 🧠 Problem

In a world where health information is abundant yet often overwhelming, individuals frequently face uncertainty when experiencing symptoms. Without immediate access to medical professionals, many resort to unreliable online sources or delay seeking care, potentially leading to worsened conditions.

---

## 💡 Solution

**MedGuide** empowers users to:

- **Describe symptoms naturally** in plain language.
- Receive **AI-generated medical guidance**, including:
  - Concise **summary** of their condition.
  - **Probable causes** and risk levels.
  - **Red flags** that indicate urgent attention.
  - **Recommended actions** and preventive tips.
- **Download PDF reports** summarizing the AI analysis.
- Enjoy a **modern, responsive, interactive interface** with animated elements.

The app leverages **Google Gemini API** to understand and process user input, producing intelligent insights in real-time.

---

## ⚙️ Implementation

MedGuide is a **frontend-only web application**:

- **Symptom Input:** Users type health concerns in a text area.
- **AI Analysis:** Input is sent to **Google Gemini API**, returning structured responses.
- **Interactive Cards:** Color-coded cards display summary, probable causes, red flags, and recommended actions.
- **Animations:** Smooth 2D transitions enhance user experience.
- **PDF Reports:** Users can download AI-generated reports for offline access.
- **UI/UX:** Light blue and light green theme with responsive design ensures accessibility and professional appearance.

This combination of AI intelligence, responsive design, and interactive elements ensures a **hackathon-ready experience** with social impact and user engagement.
